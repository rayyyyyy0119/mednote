# Cardiology Procedures and Devices

> [!warning] 使用範圍
> 本頁是查找索引，不是操作手冊。侵入性 procedure、device troubleshooting 與 mechanical support 應由受訓團隊依院內 protocol 執行。

## 快速導航

| Need | Tool／procedure | Bedside question |
|---|---|---|
| Structural／hemodynamic assessment | TTE／TEE、focused cardiac US | 影像是否回答 ventricular function、valve、effusion、pressure／volume question？ |
| Coronary definition／treatment | coronary angiography／PCI、OCT／IVUS、physiology assessment | ACS urgency、renal／bleeding risk、access、contrast、antithrombotic plan？ |
| Rhythm monitoring | telemetry、Holter／patch、implantable loop recorder | symptom frequency、syncope risk、need for long-term monitoring？ |
| Brady／tachy therapy | temporary pacing、pacemaker、ICD／CRT、ablation | reversible cause？device indication？infection／lead issue？ |
| Valve intervention | balloon valvuloplasty、TAVI／SAVR、TEER／surgery | severity、symptoms、anatomy、Heart Team decision？ |
| Temporary circulatory support | IABP、Impella-class device、TandemHeart、VA-ECMO／surgical pump | shock phenotype、goal、contraindication、exit strategy？ |

## Device patient red flags

- Syncope、shock、new HF、bradycardia／tachycardia、device alarm。
- Pocket erythema／drainage、fever／bacteremia。
- Changed mechanical valve sound、embolism、hemolysis、new murmur。
- Mechanical support：loss of flow、limb ischemia、bleeding、hemolysis、stroke。
