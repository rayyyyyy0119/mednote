# 心臟內科 Cardiology

> [!warning] 使用範圍
> 學習與臨床工作輔助用，不能取代院內 ACS／arrhythmia／heart failure protocol、最新指引、專科會診與個別病人的臨床判斷。

## 快速入口

- 主訴：[[Chest Discomfort]] · [[Dyspnea]] · [[Syncope]]
- 缺血性心臟病：[[Acute Coronary Syndrome]] · [[Chronic Coronary Disease]]
- 心衰竭：[[Heart Failure]]
- 心律不整：[[Arrhythmia Approach]] · [[Atrial Fibrillation]]
- 結構性心臟病：[[Pericarditis]] · [[Valvular Heart Disease]]
- 工具：[[Cardiology Scores and Signs]] · [[Cardiology Procedures and Devices]]

## 臨床導航

| 情境 | 先處理 | 延伸頁 |
|---|---|---|
| 急性胸痛／疑 ACS | ABC、monitor、12-lead ECG、serial hs-cTn；不穩定或 STEMI-equivalent 立即啟動院內 pathway | [[Chest Discomfort]], [[Acute Coronary Syndrome]] |
| 急性呼吸困難／肺水腫 | 評估氧合、灌流與 congestion；找 ACS、arrhythmia、hypertensive emergency、valve catastrophe | [[Dyspnea]], [[Heart Failure]] |
| 心悸／tachycardia | 先分 unstable vs stable、narrow vs wide、regular vs irregular | [[Arrhythmia Approach]], [[Atrial Fibrillation]] |
| syncope | 先找 exertional event、abnormal ECG、structural heart disease、bleeding／PE 等高風險原因 | [[Syncope]], [[Valvular Heart Disease]] |
| pleuritic／positional chest pain | 仍須先排 ACS、PE、aortic syndrome；再評估 pericarditis／myopericarditis | [[Pericarditis]] |

## 主題地圖

### Ischemic heart disease

- [[Acute Coronary Syndrome]]
- [[Chronic Coronary Disease]]
- Backlog：SCAD、MINOCA、coronary vasospasm、DAPT、lipid management、cardiac rehabilitation。

### Heart failure and cardiomyopathy

- [[Heart Failure]]
- Backlog：cardiomyopathy、Takotsubo、mechanical circulatory support、advanced HF referral。

### Rhythm

- [[Arrhythmia Approach]]
- [[Atrial Fibrillation]]
- Backlog：AFL、SVT、VT、bradycardia、pacemaker／ICD、accessory pathway。

### Structural and vascular

- [[Pericarditis]]
- [[Valvular Heart Disease]]
- Backlog：aortic aneurysm／acute aortic syndrome、VTE、pulmonary hypertension。
